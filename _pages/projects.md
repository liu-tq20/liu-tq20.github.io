      <h3 style="margin-top: 0; font-size: 1.5rem;">Dex — An AI Health Companion for Your Desk</h3>
      <p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 10px;">
        I developed Dex, a proactive, contactless health companion designed for sedentary professionals. To address the privacy concerns of traditional cameras and the user friction of wearables, we engineered a system relying entirely on ultra-low-resolution depth sensing.
      </p>
      <p style="text-align: justify; margin-bottom: 20px;">
        We built a data pipeline that translates raw spatial signals into actionable biomechanical metrics, estimating spinal load, muscle tension, and posture balance in real-time. Instead of merely presenting historical dashboards, Dex employs predictive models to deliver timely, science-backed interventions—such as prompting specific micro-stretches before muscle stiffness sets in—effectively closing the loop in proactive human-AI interaction.
      </p>
    </div>
